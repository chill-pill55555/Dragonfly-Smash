# Dragonfly Smash

Get ready to murder the dragonfly.

Dragonfly Smash is a retro pixel-art arcade game that runs in your browser. Play as a big-fisted brawler, leap across brick platforms, and smash waves of dragonflies before they take you down. Lose all your lives and a giant pixelated character will laugh in your face.

## Features

- Pixel-art graphics with a sunset city backdrop
- Dragonflies that flash red before dive-bombing you
- Waves that get faster and more crowded as you progress
- Combo scoring for quick, back-to-back smashes
- Hearts that sometimes drop from smashed dragonflies
- Retro sound effects with a mute button
- Keyboard and on-screen controls
- A mocking game-over screen with a best score for the session

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | On-screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys or A / D | Left and right arrows |
| Jump | Up, W, or Space | Up arrow |
| Drop through a platform | Down or S | Not available |
| Smash | X, F, or J | Smash |
| Start or restart | Enter or Space | Gold button |

## How to play

1. Press **Start game**.
2. Move and jump to get within reach of a dragonfly.
3. Smash it, on the ground or in mid-air.
4. Avoid getting hit. You start with 3 lives and can hold up to 5.
5. Clear each wave to move on to a harder
